# Pathfinder 2E Chargen

Now it's time to begin assembling your character's Pathfinder 2E character sheet.

Begin by inputting the following command: `cg/review`.

`cg/review` is your friend and guidebook through the sheet generation process. **Remember that your prologue should reflect your chosen options.** See [Writing a Prologue](/wiki/resource:prologue) for more information.
